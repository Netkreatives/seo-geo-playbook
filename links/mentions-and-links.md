# Mentions and links

A site with near-zero referring domains will not be rescued by more pages. *(observed)* The work is off-site.

## Worth the time

- **Directories and launch platforms.** 20–30 relevant ones, with the same name, one-line description and logo everywhere.
- **Platform ecosystems.** App stores, marketplaces and integration listings are heavily cited by assistants.
- **Unlinked mentions.** Search `"brand" -site:yourdomain.com` and ask for the link.
- **Lists that name competitors but not you.** A 120–180 word pitch with evidence. One follow-up after a week, then stop.
- **Customer stories for tools you use.** Vendors link to them.
- **Forums, as yourself, disclosed.** Sparingly.
- **Reviews and press** from people who actually used the product.

## What gets a site burned

- Link exchanges and credit networks.
- Bought placements, paid forum "tasks", fake accounts, self-written reviews.
- Mass AI-written posts across a network of sites.
- Templated location or persona pages.

Several widely shared guides and agent skills sell or automate the items in this second list. The same sources often concede that search engines classify them as link spam.

## Junk backlinks

Small sites collect links from scraper and domain-report sites. Ignore them. Do not disavow, do not buy links to "balance" them. *(observed)*
